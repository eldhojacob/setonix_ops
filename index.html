Our Approach

Technology Platform

We are building a new generation of bioactive composites that combine mechanical strength with bioactive functionality — pairing light-cure and self-cure systems, advanced fibers, bioactive fillers, and tailored composite design to meet the demands of modern orthopaedics.

  • Light-cure & self-cure composite systems

    Flexible curing chemistries for diverse clinical workflows.

  • Bioactive fillers that support bone growth

    Promoting natural integration between material and bone.

  • Engineered fibers for superior reinforcement

    Reinforcement that delivers strength and durability under load.

  • Tailored composite design for diverse applications

    Material platforms tuned to specific clinical requirements.
